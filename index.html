Methodology

How a number earns the page.

  • Protocol B — params.protocol starts with B-; derived ramps; working set past SLC.
  • Steadiness — rails.steadiness.steady==true for publishable fio windows; non-steady hatched/labeled.
  • Cold control — primarycache=none / cache_state=cold. Warm ~36 GB/s is DRAM, not the array.
  • Calibrate ≠ story — 2026-09-11-19.41 invalidated for story; superseded by 2026-09-13-18.42.
  • mdadm HOLD — parity seqRD pins at 1.14 GB/s; raid10 reads normally; do not publish “8× slower than btrfs.”
  • Public-safe — SN labels only; no serials, by-id, or SUT IPs.
